Who it’s for

  • Charge 6: outdoor use (JBL IP68 + 1 m drop), a built-in power bank, Auracast pairing, and long hangs (up to 24 hours plus 4 hours Playtime Boost, JBL spec sheet).
  • Flex: a smaller speaker than Charge 6 (Bose: 2.06" H × 7.93" W × 3.56" D, 1.291 lb), IP67 that floats, and pair-two Stereo Mode or Party Mode via the Bose app.

Who it’s not for

  • Universal-winner hunters. There is no “best overall” here.
  • Ultralight packers who need Charge-class output (Charge 6 is about 2.11–2.18 lb per JBL) — or all-day backyard volume on Flex (Bose: up to 12 hours typical, about 3 hours at max).
  • Anyone who needs an aux jack. Charge 6: USB-C in/out, no aux/USB-A. Flex: no audio cable / no aux (Bose).

Review themes (published — not our lab)

  • Trusted Reviews on Charge 6: calls it the speaker to beat in class; notes bass could go deeper and highs could stand out more. That is their framing, not a lab result from this site.
  • TechGearLab on Flex: praises sound-for-size and durability; notes shorter battery vs some rivals and no aux; they measured 10.5 h in their test (their number, not ours).

The rival is each other — a preference fork (runtime / volume / power bank vs packability / ticket / IP67 float), not a spec knockout. We are not repeating published dollar figures as current prices.

Hidden / recurring cost

USB-C brick: Charge 6 lists a ~3 hour charge at 12–20 V / 3 A (JBL) — do not assume a wall brick is in the box. Flex lists a ~4 hour USB-C charge (Bose). Some Charge 6 packs have no cable in the box (published review note). App lock-in: JBL Auracast pairing with other JBL speakers; Bose Stereo Mode / Party Mode via the Bose app. IP ratings are manufacturer lab claims, not a field test by this site.

Wait for a sale / skip if you own

No sticky dollar figure on this site — use the Amazon check-price links. MSRP themes (~$200 Charge 6 / ~$150 Flex) are themes, not a fake sale cutoff. If you already own either speaker, skip unless runtime + volume + power bank (Charge 6) or packability + IP67 float (Flex) is the reason you would switch. Check color ASINs; limited editions differ.

Bottom line

DEPENDS — Charge 6 = runtime + volume + power bank (~$200 MSRP); Flex = packability + ~$150 MSRP + IP67 float.
