Who it’s for

  • Buyers who want Sony’s current 2026 flagship buds (SoundGuys lists a Feb 12, 2026 date).
  • People who want wireless case charging, IPX4, LDAC + LC3, 8.4 mm drivers.

Who should skip

  • XM5 owners expecting a new battery class — Sony still lists max 8 hrs NC on / 12 hrs off.
  • Anyone shopping only on price: SoundGuys notes you pay a premium for focused upgrades.

Sourced facts

  • Weight about 6.5 g per bud including M tips (Sony SG); case about 47 g.
  • Battery: 8 h NC on, 12 h NC off; talk time 5 h NC on.
  • Bluetooth 5.3; IPX4; multipoint; DSEE Extreme listed.
  • SoundGuys: improvements to ANC, sound, mics, and fit; battery and codecs largely unchanged vs XM5; XM5 owners may want to wait for a sale.

Verdict

Worth it as a first Sony flagship, or as an upgrade from XM4-and-older. A harder sell if you already own XM5 unless the live price gap is small. We have not independently measured ANC.
