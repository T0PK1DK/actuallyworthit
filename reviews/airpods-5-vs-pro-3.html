Buy AirPods 5 if

You want Apple’s open-ear fit, ambient awareness, and Apple’s claimed open-ear ANC at the lower ticket ($129 / $149 Wireless Charging Case per Apple Newsroom).

Buy Pro 3 if

You need sealed tip isolation, longer manufacturer ANC listening claims, and Pro-tier Apple features (hearing-aid path / heart-rate / Fitness hooks themes — Apple-gated).
