FAQ

Is iPhone 18 Pro a redesign vs 17 Pro?

No. 17 Pro was the aluminum-unibody / camera-plateau year (Apple Newsroom 2025-09). 18 Pro keeps that chassis class (same 150.0 × 71.9 × 8.75 mm) and spends the year on A20 Pro, variable-aperture Main, next-gen vapor chamber, C2, and battery. Colors change (burgundy / glacier vs cosmic orange / deep blue).

Is the extra $100 worth it?

DEPENDS. $1,199 vs $1,099 starting MSRP (Apple Newsroom). Worth the jump if you will use variable aperture, A20 Pro sustained performance, or the 36-hour video claim. Keep 17 Pro if last year’s triple 48MP and unibody already cover you. Not a universal upgrade.

Are the cameras the same?

Both are 48MP Fusion Main + Ultra Wide + 4×/8× Telephoto with an 18MP front in Apple’s spec framing. The 18 Pro fork is variable aperture on Fusion Main (ƒ/1.48–ƒ/4.0) plus Pro controls / Apple Reference Image (Apple Newsroom 2026-09-09). Not a unique telephoto crown on this page.

What about iPhone 18 Pro Max?

Size and battery fork: 6.9-inch, from $1,299, up to 45 hours video (Apple Newsroom). Not this 6.3-inch Pro vs last-gen Pro matchup.
