Who it’s for

  • Outdoor use: JBL lists IP68 (lab: up to 1.5 m freshwater, 30 min) and drop-proof from 1 m.
  • People who want a built-in power bank and Auracast pairing with other JBL speakers.
  • Long hangs: up to 24 hours plus 4 hours Playtime Boost, dependent on volume/content (JBL spec sheet).

Who should skip

  • Ultralight packers — about 2.11–2.18 lb (0.96–0.99 kg) per JBL.
  • People who need an aux jack or a USB-A power-out port; reviews note USB-C in/out and no cable in the box on some packs.

Sourced facts

  • 45 W total: 30 W woofer + 15 W tweeter RMS; 53×93 mm woofer, 20 mm tweeter; 56 Hz–20 kHz (−6 dB).
  • Bluetooth 5.4; USB-C in/out; 34 Wh battery; ~3 hour charge at 12–20 V / 3 A.
  • Trusted Reviews: calls it the speaker to beat in class; notes bass could go deeper and highs could stand out more.

Verdict

Worth it as a mid-ticket “party/yard/beach” speaker with a power bank. Not the most packable, and JBL’s 28-hour marketing is 24+4 Playtime Boost, not a guarantee at party volume.
