Who it’s for

  • Listeners who want Bose Immersive Audio / CustomTune and Quiet / Aware / Immersion modes (Bose listing copy).
  • People who prefer Bose’s comfort reputation over Sony’s app/codec depth.

Who should skip

  • Anyone who needs 30-hour ANC as a hard requirement — Bose lists up to 24 hours, up to 18 with Immersive Audio.
  • Shoppers who actually want the 2nd Gen Ultra (different ASIN, different battery claim).

Sourced facts

  • Bluetooth 5.3; 15-minute charge listed as up to 2.5 hours (2 hours with Immersive Audio).
  • SimpleSync with Bose soundbars listed.
  • CamelCamelCamel and retailer pages treat B0CCZ1L489 as this 1st-gen Black SKU (UPC 017817846172).

Verdict

Worth it if Bose comfort + spatial/Immersive modes are the point and you are buying the correct generation. Confirm you are not accidentally comparing 1st-gen battery numbers to a 2nd-gen listing.
