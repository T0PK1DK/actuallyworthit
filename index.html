Who this site is for

People spending real money on mid-ticket electronics — roughly earbuds, headphones, GPS, and portable speakers that often sit above $100 — who want a clear “buy / skip / wait for a sale” argument. We are not trying to rank every SKU on Amazon.

If you want a store, shop Amazon. If you want someone to tell you a $250 pair of buds is “perfect for everyone,” this is the wrong site.
