Actually Worth It exists because affiliate review sites often pretend to be shops, invent five-star quotes, and hide the money. We do the opposite.

We read manufacturer specs, Amazon listing facts, and published expert-review themes (What Hi-Fi, OutdoorGearLab, Hiking Guy, SoundGuys, JBL/Bose/Sony/Garmin product pages). We do not fabricate prices, ratings, or customer quotes. If we have not used a product ourselves, the copy is labeled as sourced draft copy.

Who it’s for: people buying mid-ticket electronics (earbuds, headphones, handheld GPS, portable speakers). Who it isn’t for: bargain-bin gadget lists, 85-inch TVs, or “top 10 must-buy” slideshows.
